Working papers

When Nonbanks Dampen and When They Amplify: A Yield-Curve View of the Nonbank Lending Channel

with Peter Bednarek, Steven Ongena, and Natalja von Westernhagen

Working paper

Abstract

We show that the nonbank lending channel is yield-curve dependent. Using German credit register data and within-firm identification, we decompose high-frequency monetary policy surprises into orthogonal short-end, medium-end, and long-end components. Nonbanks expand relative to banks after short-end surprises but contract after medium- and long-end surprises. Financial service institutions drive this sign reversal through a bank credit reallocation channel, while insurers move in the opposite direction, expanding after medium- and long-end surprises as duration-driven solvency gains support credit provision. The nonbank response therefore depends on both the yield-curve segment affected by monetary policy and the composition of the nonbank sector.

Work in progress

Banks, Nonbanks, and Geopolitical Shocks: Evidence from the Russia–Ukraine War

with Daniel te Kaat, Michaela Kesina, and Natalja von Westernhagen

In progress

Abstract

Using the Russia–Ukraine war as a natural experiment, this project examines how geopolitical stress travels through bank-nonbank networks and quantifies the role that nonbanks play in stabilizing or destabilizing credit supply to firms.
